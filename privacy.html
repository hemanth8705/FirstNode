Privacy Policy

First Node (Android app) · Effective date: October 2, 2026

In short: First Node does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Everything you create in the app stays on your device. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting services.

This Privacy Policy explains how First Node (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you use it. First Node is an alarm app that lets you set alarms, choose alarm sounds, and optionally solve a puzzle to dismiss an alarm.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not send any data to us or to any server. It does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not request the Internet permission in its release build.

The app does create and keep the following information on your device only, so that it can work:

  • Alarm settings – times, repeat days, labels you type, sound choice, volume, puzzle settings, and post-alarm reminder settings.
  • Sound pools and library – the names and order of sound pools you create, and the list of sounds in your library.
  • Audio files you choose to import – if you pick audio files with the system file picker, the app copies them into its own private storage so they keep working as alarm sounds. The files are only played by the app.

This information is not collected by us, we cannot see it, and it is not linked to your identity.

2. Permissions the app uses

The app requests Android permissions only to run alarms. None of them are used to gather personal data.

PermissionWhy it is needed
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)To show the alarm and post-alarm reminder notifications.
Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM)To fire your alarms at the exact minute you set.
Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)To show the ringing screen over the lock screen when an alarm goes off.
Foreground service, media playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK)To keep the alarm sound playing reliably while the app is in the background.
Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK)To wake the device when an alarm rings.
Vibrate (VIBRATE)To vibrate when an alarm rings.
Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)To restore your scheduled alarms after the device restarts.
Notification policy access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY)Declared by the alarm library the app uses, to help alarms sound correctly alongside Do Not Disturb settings.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or other personal data. When you import sounds, you select files yourself through the Android system file picker, and the app only receives the files you choose.

3. How information is used

The information described in Section 1 is used only to schedule and ring your alarms, play the sounds you chose, run post-alarm reminders, and show your settings inside the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

4. Where information is stored

All app data is stored locally on your device, in the app’s private storage area (app preferences and a private folder for imported sounds). Other apps cannot normally access this storage.

Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android’s built-in backup feature (Auto Backup) may include app data in your own Google account backup, and may restore it when you set up a new device. That is a feature of Android and your Google account, which we do not control and do not have access to. You can turn it off in your device’s system settings.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your information to anyone. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or other third-party tracking services. The app’s open-source libraries (for example, for audio playback, alarm scheduling, and local storage) run entirely on your device and do not send data to us or to their authors.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • delete individual alarms, sound pools, and imported sounds inside the app (deleting an imported sound also removes its copied file); or
  • delete everything by clearing the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → First Node → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

Because we do not receive or hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Children’s privacy

First Node is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device and is never transmitted by the app, which greatly limits exposure. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, and the safety of your data also depends on your device’s own security (for example, a screen lock and up-to-date software).

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app’s features change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change means the app begins collecting or sharing data, we will update this policy before that change takes effect.
